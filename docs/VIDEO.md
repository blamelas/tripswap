**Goal:** if the live demo fails, this video shows the real product without the network.
Record in 1080p, with the Mac microphone, in a clean window. **Narration: English.**

## Preparation (5 min)

1. Close everything except the browser.
2. Open https://tripswap-home.vercel.app in incognito.
3. Zoom to 110%, laptop viewport.
4. Clear `/unlocks` for the empty state.
5. Turn off notifications and the phone.
6. Record 10 seconds and check the audio.
